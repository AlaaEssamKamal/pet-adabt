The Story of Cooper

Found wandering near a quiet riverside in East Texas, Cooper wasn't just another stray. He was a gentle soul waiting for someone to notice his quiet wag and soulful eyes. When he arrived at PawHaven, he was underweight and cautious, but his transformation over the last few months has been nothing short of magical.

Cooper is the definition of "man's best friend." He doesn't just want a house; he wants a home where he can be part of the morning coffee ritual and the evening sunset walks. He has a peculiar, endearing habit of bringing you his favorite plush toy whenever you walk through the door—not for you to throw, but just for you to admire.

His foster mom says he's a "natural-born babysitter," showing incredible patience with young children and a calm, protective presence that makes everyone feel safe. He's ready to fill your home with the kind of love that only a rescued heart can give.
